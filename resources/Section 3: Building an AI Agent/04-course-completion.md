# Course complete
Congratulations on completing the n8n Quickstart. You started with an empty canvas and built your way up to a working AI agent that can look up real data and answer questions. Along the way you learned how to wire up triggers and HTTP requests, transform and merge data across nodes, handle errors before they become problems, and connect an AI model to external tools.

## Where to from here
The n8n Quickstart is designed to get you productive fast. If you want to go deeper, the n8n Foundations program covers the full platform across three courses:

- **Essentials: Your First Workflows (N8N101)** covers the canvas, core nodes, data flow, and building your first integrations from scratch.

- **Integrations: APIs and Connected Workflows (N8N102)** covers HTTP requests, webhooks, API authentication, flow logic with IF, Switch, and Loop nodes, sub-workflows, Data Tables, and error handling. All exercises use practice APIs hosted by the Academy, so no external accounts are needed.

- **In Practice: AI, Testing and Best Practices (N8N103)** covers AI-powered workflows using the AI Agent node, testing and debugging with the execution log and Try/Catch, and workflow organization with naming conventions, version control, and production readiness.
Complete all three Foundations courses and you earn the n8n Foundations program badge. An official n8n certification exam is also on the way, giving you a way to validate your skills across the full program.

## Join the community

Being part of the n8n community helps you stay up to date, get help, and share your automation projects. You can participate online, and in many regions there are also in-person meetups and events.

- **Online community** — Join discussions, ask questions, and share workflows on the official n8n forum: [community.n8n.io](https://community.n8n.io/)

- **Local meetups and events** — Community members and ambassadors host meetups where you can meet other n8n users, attend talks or workshops, and network. Upcoming events are announced through community channels and listed on our [Luma page](https://luma.com/n8n-events).
